Contextual Hate Speech Detection Using CNNs

An academic research study of CNN-based classification of social-media text into hate speech, offensive language, and neither.

Research topic

The study explores natural-language processing and convolutional neural networks for three-class classification of social-media posts, with attention to contextual meaning and the effects of class imbalance.

Dataset and methodology

The paper uses 24,783 labeled tweets and describes text preprocessing, pretrained Word2Vec embeddings, convolutional filters, pooling, and softmax classification.

Reported findings

The submitted research paper reports 91% overall accuracy, with recall and F1-score of 0.90. The discussion considers class-level behavior and the importance of evaluation beyond accuracy.

Research materials

The full academic paper, methodology, results, figures, authorship details, and research documentation are available in the dedicated repository.
