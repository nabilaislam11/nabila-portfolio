ACADEMIC STUDY · DATA AND DECISION SYSTEMS

Business Intelligence, AI & Big Data Analytics

An academic comparative study of business intelligence, artificial intelligence, and big-data analytics.

Study overview

This academic topic examines how business intelligence and artificial intelligence relate to analytical decision-making in environments involving large and varied data.

Conceptual focus

Business intelligence emphasizes reporting, dashboards, querying, and organizational insight. Artificial intelligence introduces statistical learning, predictive models, and automated pattern discovery. Big-data platforms provide methods for processing and analyzing data at scale.

Areas of comparison

The study topic connects differences in analytical methods, applications, data workflows, and the complementary roles of BI and AI in modern information systems.
